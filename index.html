Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about ordering from Henzo Farm

Do you deliver across Islamabad & Rawalpindi?
Yes! We deliver to all sectors of Islamabad, Bahria Town, DHA, Gulberg Greens, PWD, and all areas of Rawalpindi including Saddar, Satellite Town, Adiala Road, and Westridge. Free delivery on orders of Rs 2,000 and above.
How do chick pre-orders work?
Day-old chicks are booked in advance and come from our next hatch. When you place a chick order, it's a pre-order — we prepare your chicks and have them ready for collection or delivery about 1 week (7 days) after you order. We'll confirm the exact ready date with you on WhatsApp. Eggs, mature hens, roosters and breeding pairs are available from ready stock without the wait.
Are your chickens vaccinated?
Absolutely. All our chicks receive standard vaccinations (Newcastle, Infectious Bronchitis, Gumboro). We provide vaccination records with every purchase. Our mature hens and roosters are also health-checked before delivery.
What makes your eggs different from store-bought?
Our hens are free-range on natural feed with no chemicals or hormones, and the deep golden yolk comes from natural feed ingredients — not synthetic colorants. You'll notice the richer flavor and firmer whites. Many customers say our eggs taste like "eggs used to taste" — real farm freshness.
Can I visit the farm before buying?
Yes! We welcome farm visits at Henzo Farm, Islamabad. Please call or WhatsApp us at +92 311 5190548 to schedule a visit. Best times are Saturday and Sunday mornings between 8 AM and 12 PM.
What payment methods do you accept?
We accept Cash on Delivery (COD) for all orders across Islamabad and Rawalpindi. For bulk orders or breeding pairs, we also accept bank transfer. Payment is collected at the time of delivery.

About Henzo Farm

Henzo Farm was founded by Maaz Mustafa Minhas with a simple mission: to bring back the tradition of pure, heritage poultry breeding in Islamabad.

Located in the pristine foothills of Pindorian Shareef, our farm specializes in two world-class heritage breeds — the record-breaking Black Australorp and the resilient Rhode Island Red.

From day-old chicks to mature laying hens, from breeding pairs to farm-fresh organic eggs, we deliver quality you can taste and trust. Every egg has a golden yolk. Every bird is raised with integrity.

"Healthy Birds. Healthy Life."
